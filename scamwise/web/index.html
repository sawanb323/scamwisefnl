    Product

    Built around how scams actually work

    Fake KYC updates, “digital arrests”, parcel and electricity-bill scams, task jobs and OTP theft all rely on pressure. SCAMWISE spots the pressure and shows you exactly where it is.

    Transparent risk score

    High, medium, low or inconclusive, with every point of the score tied to a named warning sign: urgency, OTP requests, payment demands, impersonation, link tricks.

    Codes stay on your device

    OTPs, card numbers, passwords and Aadhaar-style numbers are removed in your browser before anything is sent. Linked devices only learn a code was present.

    Family alerts in real time

    Link a parent's phone or your laptop. When one device checks something high-risk, the others are alerted within seconds.

    Link checks without clicking

    Look-alike domains, shorteners, raw IP addresses and .apk downloads are flagged, and links are checked against the URLhaus malware database. SCAMWISE never opens them.

    Only what you paste

    No app permissions and no access to your inbox. SCAMWISE sees the text you choose to check and nothing else.

    Approved devices only

    A new device needs a single-use code and approval from a device you already use. Unlink any device instantly from anywhere.

    Privacy & security

    Designed to hold as little as possible

    Every request is checked by the database itself with row-level security. Each device is bound to its own sign-in session, and unlinking a device ends that session on the server immediately.

    What we store

    • Risk level and score
    • Warning types and link domains
    • Time and the name of the device that checked it
    • Deleted automatically after 7, 30 or 90 days, your choice

    What we never store

    • The message itself (unless you opt in to a short masked excerpt)
    • Any OTP, PIN, card number or password
    • Your contacts or other apps' data
    • Anything you didn't paste

    FAQ

    Questions

    Still unsure? Send us a message.

    Can SCAMWISE read my SMS or WhatsApp?

    No. A website cannot read your messages or other apps. SCAMWISE only sees the text you choose to paste into it.

    If SCAMWISE says “Low risk”, is the message safe?

    Not necessarily. Low means no strong scam pattern was found. New scams appear every day, so always verify payment or account requests through the official app or number.

    What does “Inconclusive” mean?

    There wasn't enough evidence either way, for example a bare link that could not be checked against threat intelligence. Treat it with caution.

    What happens to an OTP in a message I check?

    It is replaced with [REDACTED] in your browser before anything is sent. Your linked devices get an alert saying a code was exposed, never the code itself.

    Does it work on iPhone and laptops?

    Yes. SCAMWISE runs in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac. There is nothing to install.

    I already lost money. What should I do?

    Call your bank immediately, then call the national cyber-crime helpline 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in. Speed matters for freezing funds.
